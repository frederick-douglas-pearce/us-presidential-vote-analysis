# CLAUDE.md — `src/usvote/pv/`

Module-specific guidance, moved from the root `CLAUDE.md` (#298). Claude Code loads this file on demand, once a file under this directory is read. The root file's module map and cross-cutting invariants still apply.

## Shared PV contracts

Two shared, **source-neutral** things sit between them — the `usvote/pv/` package and the `usvote/years.py` module — and the dependency always runs `source -> shared`, never source-to-source. `usvote/pv/` holds the contracts both PV sources conform to — `schema.py` (the D018 record shape + `dwh.pv_votes` DDL), `status.py` (the D024 `pv_state_status` roster shape, the `pv_status` enum, and the two-way roster/fact assert), `validate.py` (the frame invariants both sources assert — `assert_pv_grain` and `assert_totals_not_exceeded`, #82; kept out of `schema.py` because they constrain data *values*, not the D018 column/DDL contract), `views.py` (the three D017 resolution views over the raw union), and `load.py` (the two write seams, `load_pv_records` for the fact and `load_pv_status` for the roster).

## `overlap.py` — the D017 layer-3 gate

`usvote/pv/overlap.py` (#167 / D051) is the **D017 layer-3 gate** — the tolerance D017 §3 declared and never shipped, calibrated by #70 and adopted as D051. It sits on `validate.py`'s shelf (invariants over data *values*) and belongs under `usvote/pv/` for the same reason the roster contract does: it compares two sources, so neither source's subpackage can own it (D015). Two of D051's three thresholds live here — gate 1's exact-match-rate floor (≥90% overall, ≥80% any year) and gate 2's per-cell relative ceiling (flag at 1% → the D005 list, fail at 15%), both at the `(year, state, candidate)` cell grain over `pv_redistributable` vs `pv_ucsb`, **never** the raw union. **Gate 3's placement is forced, not preferred**: the national margin-difference ceiling (≤0.25 pp) derives its margin with `roll_up_national`, so a home here would need the `pv -> hybrid` import `test_layering.py` forbids — it lives in `hybrid.py` beside the computation it protects. The relative-delta basis is `|MIT − UCSB| ÷ **UCSB**`, asymmetric and therefore part of the decision rather than an implementation detail. **No magnitude leaves the module**: `OverlapKey` carries `(year, state, candidate)` plus party for display and has no numeric field, which makes D030/D022 structural rather than a matter of discipline — MIT is CC0 and exactly reproducible, so an attributed *relative* delta inverts to the UCSB integer. **A year only one source reaches is excluded only at or beyond the spine frontier** — `max(ec_ingest_years())`, the newest election the EC spine knows about (`pv -> years`, the direction `pv/absences.py` already takes). At the frontier, one source publishing first is ordinary asymmetric refresh (MIT is a CSV drop, the UCSB corpus a manual snapshot), and the bound is inclusive because the frontier year is precisely the one legitimately one-sided mid-cycle. **Below** it, a one-sided year is a regression: it stays in the population, reads 0%, and trips gate 1's per-year floor. **The frontier is the spine's, not the data's, and that is the point** — an earlier form took it from the frames themselves (`min(max MIT year, max UCSB year)`) and was *circular*, letting the data define the boundary meant to detect the data being wrong: a MIT CSV truncated at 1996 pulled the ceiling down with it, excluded seven elections and reported **100% agreement** on the remainder while 2000–2024 vanished into the snapshot and the public API. Deriving it from `ec_ingest_years()` rather than `LATEST_ELECTION_YEAR` means a year gated back into `UNSUPPORTED_EC_YEARS` moves the frontier down with it, and adds no new thing to maintain — the cycle bump the spine already needs moves this too. Gates 1–2 are the **only** instrument pointed at a *MIT* coverage hole, since MIT's D024 roster derives its in-scope years from the rows MIT actually loaded; **UCSB is not symmetric** — it derives scope from the spine and `_scope_years` raises on a missing page before the warehouse is built. The stated limit is a source dropping a year **at or beyond the frontier** — MIT losing 2024 is excluded by the very clause that lets a mid-refresh 2024 through, so that year's popular vote goes silently null (the year itself still ships; the join is EC-left) and the gate stays green; nothing *below* the frontier is ever excluded, whatever else the source carries. Its owner is a guard beside MIT's own invariants rather than a second clause here, and as of #177/D052 that guard exists: `usvote/mit/validate.py`'s `assert_mit_year_coverage` refuses such a CSV at the MIT read seam (raw frame, pre-`years`-filter; `validate_coverage` defaults **off** at `run_mit_pipeline` and **on** at `run_warehouse`) — **D052 carries why it is two checks, why the floor stays with `assert_mit_year_floor`, and the limit no mechanical check closes.** Both gates **skip, never fail**, when UCSB is absent, so a public EC + MIT clone is unaffected — which also means the gate cannot run in CI (D022) and its acceptance evidence is local-only.

## `absences.py` — the curated absence catalog

`usvote/pv/absences.py` (E8-S9 prereq, #140) is the one module under `usvote/pv/` that carries **data and a derivation** rather than a contract, and it belongs there for the same reason the roster contract does: which states held no popular vote is a fact about the *election*, source-neutral. It holds `PV_ABSENCE_CATALOG` — the **32 pre-1976 `(year, state)` absences** (18 `legislature_chosen` + 14 `not_participating`), each a `PVAbsence(pv_status, citation)` carrying a **public-domain** citation (*McPherson v. Blacker*, 146 U.S. 1 (1892) for the 1824/1828-1860 legislative appointments; Colo. Const. of 1876 Schedule § 19 for Colorado; the H.R. 126 joint resolution for 1864). It exists because the pre-1976 classifications previously had exactly one origin — parsing UCSB's markup — and UCSB grants no reuse rights (D022) while the snapshot is redistributable-only *at the source* (D030), so a public `pv_status` back to 1824 (#139) cannot be built on UCSB-derived rows. The **derivation is single-sourced in `status.py`**: `build_roster(..., absences=...)` takes the map and `popular_vote` is the **residual**; `build_popular_vote_roster` is the empty-map call (MIT) and `build_curated_roster` is the catalog-bound one, so the dependency runs `absences -> status` and `status.py` still imports nothing from `usvote`. Two properties are enforced rather than conventional: `CURATED_YEARS = frozenset(ec_ingest_years())` (with a count pin) makes scope explicit so the catalog's *silence* about an unreviewed year cannot read as "no absences" — `build_curated_roster` **raises** outside it. That pin is what made #143 (ingesting 1868) a deliberate act: the four 1868 rows were curated here in #140 while the year was still gated, so admitting them needed **no new research**, only lifting `UNSUPPORTED_EC_YEARS` and bumping `CURATED_YEAR_COUNT` 49 → 50 (and again 50 → 51 in #144, where 1872 was reviewed and found to have **no** absences — a finding, not a silence, though the corroboration is the UCSB cross-source control test rather than the spine: with AR/LA's allotments restored the year has no zero-EV state, so the spine check inspects nothing there, and it is blind to `legislature_chosen` absences in any case since those states appointed electors); and `assert_catalog_matches_spine` cross-checks against the EC spine in **both** directions, the second (no zero-EV spine state may be left `popular_vote`) being the one with teeth, since the residual would otherwise absorb a newly-zero-EV state silently. `tests/unit/test_layering.py` proves the firewall structurally in both directions — the catalog imports/reads nothing UCSB (proved in a subprocess with `usvote.ucsb` made unimportable, not grepped), and nothing under `usvote/ucsb/` imports the catalog, which is what keeps `TestRealCorpus`'s cross-source control test non-circular: **UCSB validates the catalog, never feeds it**. That control test agrees on the **classification** of all 2,204 `(year, state)` pairs across 51 years — note that the shared *membership* is the design, not a finding, since D024 §6 has both sources derive it from the same EC spine, so what it independently corroborates is the `pv_status`, above all the 32 absences — and it **skips in CI** (D022), so running it with `USVOTE_UCSB_HTML_DIR` set is a merge precondition; the 14 `not_participating` rows are the CI-provable subset, verified both ways against the committed zero-EV fixture. Catalogued in [`docs/corrections.md`](../../../docs/corrections.md), whose third section states plainly that it is **not a correction to anybody's data**.
